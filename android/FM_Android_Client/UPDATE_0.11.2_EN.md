# Farm Manager 0.11.2 — agreed greetings

Install `dist/FM-Client-0.11.2-debug.apk` over 0.11.1, without uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

The greeting is chosen by the phone's local time. The wording is kept word for word;
in the intro the first part and the continuation are shown as two text blocks. With an enlarged
system font, a long continuation may wrap onto an additional line.

| Local time | Greeting |
|---|---|
| 06:00–11:59 | Good morning and may everything start smoothly! |
| 12:00–17:59 | Have a good day and may your results be precise! |
| 18:00–23:59 | Have a good evening and may your plans take shape! |
| 00:00–05:59 | Good night and may everything run steadily! |

The phrase is updated at launch and when returning to an intro that has not finished yet.
The fix for the large system icon from 0.11.1 is kept. The animation, background, continue button,
inventory functions and settings have not changed. The previous FM icon remains on the home screen.

After the update, fully close and reopen the app to see the intro.
On the phone, check the wrapping of the long morning phrase, especially with an enlarged system font.
