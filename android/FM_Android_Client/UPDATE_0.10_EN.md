# Farm Manager 0.10 — appearance

## Installation

1. Copy `dist/FM-Client-0.10.0-debug.apk` to the phone and open it.
2. Choose **Update**. Do not uninstall the installed version 0.9 and do not clear its data.
3. Open **Farm Manager**. After the restart, sign in to FM as usual: the password and session
   are deliberately not saved to storage. The address and the app's local data are kept.

The inventory module and the FM core do not need to be updated. QR codes do not need to be reprinted either.

## What has changed

- Header A: **Farm Manager** on the left, the original **3diwell** logo on the right.
- The logo's brand white and orange colors are kept. In the light theme it sits on a small
  dark background; the trademark itself is not recolored or stretched.
- Icon No. 3, **"Glass"**; the geometry of the original mark is kept. A regular shape without a round background
  added by the app. The Xiaomi launcher/theme may apply its own styling.
- The name of the installed app is **Farm Manager**.
- The slogan "Your farm. Always at hand." has been removed; the sign-in heading is "Connect to FM".
  The version number has moved to the bottom. The HTTP warning is kept.

## Quick check on the phone

1. Check the new name and icon on the home screen. If the old picture is still there,
   try restarting the phone; there is no need to uninstall the app because of the icon.
2. The sign-in screen has no old slogan and no repeated FM. The white-and-orange 3diwell is fully readable.
3. After signing in, check both themes via "More", rotation and an enlarged system font:
   neither the name nor the logo may be cut off. With a large font, the header may take two lines.
4. Make sure the inventory, spool and label lists are available. The logic of receiving, NFC,
   installation/replacement/removal and choosing between neighboring QR codes is unchanged.

This is a test APK with the same signature as before. The automated checks have passed; the display
on Xiaomi in this build still requires your check.
