# FM Android 0.15.8 — new icons and theme colors

Install dist/FM-Client-0.15.8-debug.apk over the current app,
without uninstalling it or clearing its data.

The new FM-icons-v3 set has been applied to native buttons, tabs and cards.
In the dark theme, the color of active icons is a calm silver #C9CDD3,
in the light theme — wet asphalt #4F565D. Both layers of the drawing are the same color,
without the former light-blue and orange parts. The backgrounds and text of selected tabs
keep the FM styling. Filled buttons use contrasting
white/dark icons; unavailable buttons are distinguished by a muted color.

Projects, inventory, "More", sending to print and PDF download have received their own
symbols. The printer camera is a video camera, the scanner is a QR code in four corners,
repeat is a circular arrow with a start symbol inside. Simplified variants are provided
for 20–24dp; the large QR button uses the detailed drawing.

At the owner's request, the 3D inventory hints are kept exactly as they were:
printer, full/empty spool and warehouse. Their images, color, sequence,
captions and step highlighting have not changed. The logo and printer photos are kept.

Check on the phone:
- refresh, the bell, sorting, QR and the video camera on the printers tab;
- pause/resume/stop: an unavailable command must be muted;
- the send icon for project and task files;
- the light and dark themes, the selected tab and a large font;
- the inventory operation diagrams: their 3D images must stay the same.

Module/rights rules, server checks, command confirmations, start accounting,
PrintJournal, connections and inventory operations have not changed. No changes are made
to the core or modules. Real hardware was not used during development.
